Pastry Trivia

When did pasteries first appear?

The earliest forms of pastry first appeared in Ancient Egypt. These primitive pastries were dense, bread-like mixtures made from grains and water, sweetened with honey,and filled with fruits or nuts 2600 BC. (Source: Uncultured Palate)
